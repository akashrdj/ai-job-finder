1-Click Automation

🚀 Job Application 1-Click Autofill

Automatically fills your name, email, phone number, location, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, PES University education, and standard authorization answers on:

Greenhouse • Lever • Ashby • Workday • LinkedIn Easy Apply

How to Install:

  1. Make sure your browser's Bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl + Shift + B).
  2. Drag and drop this blue button directly into your Bookmarks Bar:
⚡ Autofill Job Application

How to Use:

  1. Click any job's "Apply on Company Portal" button in your AI Job Finder dashboard.
  2. When the application form opens on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or LinkedIn, click ⚡ Autofill Job Application in your Bookmarks bar.
  3. Watch all inputs populate in under 1 second. Attach your resume PDF and submit!
